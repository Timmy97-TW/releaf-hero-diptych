# ReLeaf homepage diptych

Working page for the ReLeaf (GEMS Taiwan, iGEM 2026) homepage hero: how the photo of the team walking behind Farmer Chen into her field pairs with a staged lab shot of the assembled bioreactor.

- The left photo's perspective, measured (vanishing point, horizon, logo position)
- Why the current hero layout cannot hold this photo
- Four ways to join the two photos, with live mockups
- A shot plan for the lab photo: drawing, floor plan, spec sheet, shoot-day checklist

The lab half is a drawing built from the measured perspective, to be replaced by the real photo.
